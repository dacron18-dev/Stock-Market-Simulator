# Stock Market Simulator System — Problem Statement

A user wants to learn and practice stock trading without risking real money. The Stock Market Simulator allows users to create an account, receive virtual money, view simulated stock prices, place buy and sell orders, and monitor their portfolio.

The system validates each order against the user's available virtual cash or stock holdings before executing it. A simulation clock periodically advances the market, causing stock prices to change according to simulated market conditions and market events. Users can view their holdings, cash balance, transaction history, and portfolio performance. Users can also configure price alerts for stocks they are interested in.

An administrator can start or stop the simulation, configure simulation parameters, and reset the simulated market. The system provides an educational and realistic environment for understanding stock-market trading without involving real money or real financial transactions.
